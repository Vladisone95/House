How to read this

  • Low = DIY-store / IKEA ranges, melamine carpentry · Mid = typical Bucharest workshop, painted MDF, mid brands · High = premium custom carpentry and brands.
  • Surfaces the variant keeps as delivered cost nothing. Changed tiles / parquet cost only the material above the developer's budget per m² (the developer still lays them); green lines are developer-supplied pieces deducted from what you choose, so a better shower or basin costs only the difference.
  • Quantities are measured from the 3D model; unit prices are estimates, not quotes - replace them in data/prices.toml with real offers (marked quote).
  • Kitchen wall tiles (backsplash) are not part of the standard delivery.
  • Your own things, not counted: 16 props (tableware, PC, shoes...).
  • Not included: design fees, moving, the notary / bank costs of the purchase.
